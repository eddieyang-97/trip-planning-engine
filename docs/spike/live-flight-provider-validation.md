# Live flight provider validation

9 October 2026 · Public-document validation; no credentialed flight search performed

Implementation follow-up: a [standalone six-search probe](../flight-probe.md) is now implemented and offline-tested. It still needs an authorized Free SerpApi key; no live evidence has been obtained. The production rights gates proposed below concern a durable service used through assistants. They do not supersede the separate private, manually curated [policy-note workflow](../policy-notes.md).

## Recommendation and decision

**No-go for the current automated, baggage-complete hero scenario. Conditional choice: SerpApi for the smallest flight-discovery capability probe, not a committed production provider.** No reviewed option establishes all three requirements together: practical pilot access, permission for durable evidence and assistant redisplay, and a four-traveler easyJet round trip with quoted snowboard inventory and price in both directions. This is an evidence gap, not a claim that no supplier could ever meet the requirement.

Preserve the persistent decision engine. If rights or ancillaries cannot be cleared, ship a **saved travel research workspace with manual evidence**, with optional live discovery only after its separate rights gate passes. Show flight observations, seller links and unresolved costs; require a user-observed airline quote for equipment and complete party cost. Repeat comparisons concern comparable dated observations, not guaranteed availability. Do not advertise automatic baggage-complete ranking, comprehensive London coverage, or a verified live research product while manual-only. This is a proposed product rescope; no PRD or application changes were made here.

Read the specified PRD, original spike research/README, implementation/account-linking reports and implemented provider/domain/acceptance types. The service remains one Free Render service plus Supabase. Its health, fixtures and historical Trip.com connector observation do not prove backend supplier access. No accounts, subscriptions, contacts, bookings, deployments, authentication changes or other-file edits were performed.

Evidence labels below: **D** = expressly documented; **I** = interpretation/inference, not permission; **U** = unresolved contract or runtime fact. Public response examples are **samples**, not observations for Flaine. Prices are published USD charges before tax unless specified. Recheck terms before any later activation.

### Airline rules are context, not quote completion

easyJet's [sports policy](https://www.easyjet.com/en/help/baggage/sports-equipment) classifies snowboards as small sports equipment (20kg maximum), permits one small item per person and disallows pooling sports/hold-bag weight. Its readable [fees table](https://www.easyjet.com/en/terms-and-conditions/fees?sc_customer=122705607) lists £42 online / £47 customer-services for small sports equipment **per item per flight**. The canonical fees URL redirected to an unreadable help-center body in this retrieval, so retain the accessible table URL and recheck at test time.

For one board on a nonstop easyJet flight each way, £42 + £42 = **£84 policy-based estimate**, not a quoted available add-on. Do not populate `snowboardAllowed=yes` or `snowboardTotalMinor=8400` from this calculation. A connecting itinerary may require different segment/supplier handling. [Cabin rules](https://www.easyjet.com/en/help/baggage/cabin-bags) include a free small underseat bag (45×36×20cm, up to 15kg); larger bags depend on fare/purchase and availability. [Hold luggage](https://www.easyjet.com/en/help/baggage/hold-luggage) supports purchased 15/23kg bags and weight upgrades. None establishes the selected fare's inventory or required bag price. Carry-on counts never establish snowboard coverage.

## Bounded searches-per-trip economics

Proposed pilot settings, **not enabled**: two user-triggered runs per trip (initial + one repeat), max 14 potentially metered requests/run, **28/trip**, max three expanded candidate pairs/run and at most 20 normalized observations. No background polling searches, calendar fan-out or unlimited retries. This replaces the original optimistic ten-call estimate when diagnosing all six airports.

| Work per run | Maximum requests |
|---|---:|
| Multi-origin discovery (investigate different London return airports) | 1 |
| Six isolated airport diagnostics | 6 |
| Selected outbound return expansions | 3 |
| Seller/fare/booking-option calls for three complete pairs | 3 |
| Explicit retry or targeted refresh reserve, within the same cap | 1 |
| **Run / two-run trip ceiling** | **14 / 28** |

Querying all airports is not fully expanding every result. Report `complete=false` and the unexpanded/truncated scope. If the selected option is outside the three-expansion allowance, replace an expansion slot or disclose the need for another authorized run; never fabricate its return or total. There is no equipment-call budget because no usable equipment endpoint has been established.

At published [SerpApi pricing](https://serpapi.com/pricing), allocated Starter cost is $25/1,000 = **$0.025/request**, at most **$0.35/run or $0.70/trip** at full quota utilization. Free supports floor(250/28) = **8 capped trips/month** (224 calls); Starter supports **35** (980 calls), before other usage. A dedicated Starter subscription used for one trip still costs $25, not $0.70. Cache discounts can improve actual consumption but are not the budget premise. Proposal: free-only test, reserve ≤200 monthly provider credits globally (leaving 50), ≤28 per pilot owner/trip, stop at limit; monthly paid spend ceiling **$0 unless separately authorized**.

SearchApi's allocated $0.004 gives $0.112 for 28 analogous calls, but its $40 subscription is the actual entry cost and equivalent call semantics have not been proved. LetsFG's listed $0.01 would give $0.28 for 28 chargeable searches, but its flow and rights are unverified. Duffel's arithmetic with zero orders would give 28×$0.005 = $0.14 excess search, **not permission or a viable external-booking product**. Skyscanner/Amadeus lack a deployable tariff here. These comparisons exclude infrastructure, tax, currency conversion, manual work and a future ancillary supplier.

Before issuing requests reserve credits in durable run accounting. Record attempted, supplier-accepted, metered and reconciled counts separately. SerpApi request IDs support archive reconciliation when received; a lost response without an ID remains `outcome_unknown`. No upstream idempotency guarantee was found. Fence late responses; never let an expired local lease automatically reissue a potentially metered request. Reserve retries conservatively even where vendor errors are ordinarily uncharged.

## Exact blockers and next action

4. **Implementation:** provenance, retention, fresh-versus-expiring observation semantics, handoff method, per-leg ancillary evidence and safe metered-request reconciliation are not supported by the current fixture-only boundary.

**Next actionable technical test:** the six-request LGW SerpApi probe using an existing authorized free key, then the bounded all-six-airport checks if it returns useful evidence. **Next production gate:** review written rights covering the exact engine workflow before enabling durable provider evidence; supplier contact is outside this task's authorization.
